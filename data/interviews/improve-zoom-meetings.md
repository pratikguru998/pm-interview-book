::candidate 00:55
**Causes of meeting fatigue** (Stanford research on "Zoom fatigue" pointed to constant eye contact, seeing yourself, reduced mobility and higher mental effort):
- Too many meetings, back-to-back, no breaks.
- Meetings without a purpose or agenda.
- Being on camera constantly (self-view, appearance pressure).
- Attending just to stay informed.
- Long meetings with low engagement.

**Segments:** individual contributors (attend many meetings "just in case"), managers (back-to-back), cross-time-zone teams (meetings at bad hours).

Focus: **ICs attending meetings to stay informed**. A large share of meetings could be async for them.

::candidate 01:30
**Pains:** "I attend 25 meetings a week; maybe 8 need me." Fear of missing decisions if they skip. Can't multitask well. Camera-on fatigue.

**Prioritize:** making it **safe to skip** (because you can catch up quickly) is the highest leverage. It removes attendees, and the meeting becomes smaller and more focused.

::candidate 01:50
**Ideas:**
1. **AI meeting recap:** summary, decisions, action items with owners, and highlights you were mentioned in, delivered 5 minutes after the meeting; searchable.
2. **"Optional" RSVP + async catch-up:** mark yourself as "catch up later"; get the recap + key clips.
3. **Agenda nudges:** meetings without an agenda get a prompt; AI drafts one from the invite.
4. **Default 25/50-minute meetings** with automatic breaks between back-to-back meetings.
5. **Camera-off norms:** hide self-view by default, avatars, "audio-only" meeting templates.
6. **Async video updates (Zoom Clips):** replace status meetings with short recordings.

::interviewer
Isn't the AI recap available from competitors too?

::candidate 02:20
Yes, recaps are becoming table stakes, so it's not a moat alone. The differentiation is in **workflow around it**: "catch up later" RSVPs that hosts see, personalized recaps ("what matters to *you*"), action items synced to project tools, and team-level insights for admins ("your org spends 30% of meeting time in recurring meetings with no agenda"). That combination makes Zoom the *meeting-quality* platform rather than just a video pipe.

::candidate 02:50
**Recommendation:** AI recap + "catch up later" RSVP flow + agenda nudges, with admin insights for companies.

**Metrics:**
- **Value:** meeting satisfaction score (quick post-meeting rating), % of meetings with agendas, attendees per recurring meeting (should drop), recap views by non-attendees.
- **Business North Star for this initiative:** seat retention / net revenue retention for customers using these features.
- **Guardrails:** decisions missed (survey), recap accuracy (evals), privacy concerns about recording.

**Summary:** Meeting fatigue comes from too many meetings people attend just to stay informed. Making it safe to skip, with personalized AI recaps and a "catch up later" RSVP, plus agenda nudges, shrinks meetings and increases their value. Measured by satisfaction and seat retention rather than minutes.
