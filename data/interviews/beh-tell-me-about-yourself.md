This must be **your** story. Below is a model to adapt. Structure: **Present** (current role + one quantified win) → **Past** (how you got here, key strengths) → **Future** (why this role). About 90 seconds. Practise it until it sounds natural, not memorized.

::interviewer 00:00
Tell me about yourself.

::candidate 00:05
**Present.** I'm currently a product manager at a fintech company, where I lead the onboarding and activation area for our consumer app. Over the last year, my team redesigned identity verification and the first-deposit flow, which increased activation (users making their first transaction within 7 days) from **30% to 42%**.

**Past.** I started my career as a software engineer, building backend services for payments. That's where I learned to work closely with engineers and understand technical trade-offs. I gradually moved toward product: I started running user interviews for my team, then took on PM responsibilities for a small feature, and moved into a full PM role three years ago. Along the way, I've found my strengths are **turning ambiguous problems into clear plans**, using **data and user research** together, and building strong partnerships with engineering.

**Future.** I'm excited about this role because your team is focused on **helping small businesses get paid faster**. I've seen that problem up close: my parents run a small shop. The role combines activation and growth challenges similar to my current work with a larger scale and a B2B angle I want to learn. I'd love to bring my onboarding experience to your merchant activation goals.

::interviewer
What's one thing you'd want to improve about yourself as a PM?

::candidate 01:20
I'm working on **delegating discovery work earlier**. I tend to want to do all the user interviews myself because I learn so much from them. But on a larger team, it's better to involve designers and engineers directly, so they build their own user empathy and I'm not a bottleneck. Recently I started rotating interviews across the team, and it improved our solution ideas noticeably.

::coach
