# 🧾 Unit economics: CAC, LTV, payback, margins

> **In one line:** do you make more from a customer than it costs to get and serve them?

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  CAC["💸 CAC<br/>cost to acquire one customer"] --> C{"LTV : CAC"}
  LTV["💰 LTV<br/>gross profit over customer lifetime"] --> C
  C -->|"< 1"| X["🔥 Losing money on each customer"]
  C -->|"~3"| G["✅ Healthy"]
  C -->|"> 5"| U["🚀 Under-investing in growth?"]
```

## Formulas

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| **CAC** | sales & marketing spend ÷ new customers |
| **ARPU** | revenue ÷ active users (per month) |
| **Gross margin** | (revenue − cost of goods sold) ÷ revenue |
| **Churn (monthly)** | customers lost ÷ customers at the start of the month |
| **LTV** | ARPU × gross margin ÷ monthly churn |
| **Payback period** | CAC ÷ (ARPU × gross margin) months |

## Levers to improve unit economics

```mermaid
mindmap
  root((Better unit economics))
    Lower CAC
      Referrals
      SEO / content
      Better conversion
    Higher ARPU
      Upsell tiers
      Price increase
      Add-ons
    Higher margin
      Lower infrastructure cost
      Automation
    Lower churn
      Onboarding
      Annual plans
      Engagement
```
